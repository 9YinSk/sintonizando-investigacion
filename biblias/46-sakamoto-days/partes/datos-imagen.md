## Taro Sakamoto · Appearance (texto de la wiki)

Sakamoto is a tall, fat man with slicked back gray hair that is tied into a ponytail at the back of his head (similar to a man bun) and he has a small broom mustache. He typically wears an apron over a T-shirt and long pants. Sakamoto prefers to wear slippers, even when outside. His eyes are usually hidden by the glare of his round glasses. In the past, Sakamoto was much leaner with no mustache. His facial features were more defined and his eyes were visible through his glasses. Through burning through his accumulated calories, Sakamoto can revert to this form for a power boost. Shin notes his transformed appearance is very handsome, as he was noted for his handsomeness in the past. During the JCC infiltration arc, Nagumo disguised Sakamoto as his wife Aoi Sakamoto. ## Lu Wutang · Appearance (texto de la wiki)
_Fuente: https://sakamoto-days.fandom.com/wiki/Lu_Wutang#Appearance_

Lu Wutang is a handsome Chinese young man of tall and slim build. He has olive green eyes and black short hair that is tied in a French braid with red holders and finished with a red ribbon. He sports a purple diamond tattoo underneath his left eye and wears red earrings with seafoam green bells on both side of his ears and all his fingers having seafoam green rings on his left hand. His outfit consists in a lilac Changshan with dark purple lines, dark purple pants and black shoes. Wutang usually wears a dark purple jacket with a yellow distorted line on it and yellow lines in its sleeves, in the manga the jacket included a pair of chains as a collar, but it was omitted in the anime. As a child, Wutang didn´t have the French braid and wears a blue t-shirt.
