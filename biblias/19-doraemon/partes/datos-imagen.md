Doraemon is a blue robot cat from the 22nd century. He has white circular palms and feet, a large white face with six whiskers and a red nose, a red collar with a yellow bell attached, a red tail which acts as a power lever, and his 4D Pocket on his belly. Although being made up of metal, he can be squeezed and stretched. As he does not have any ears, there is a running gag where he is mistaken for a Japanese raccoon dog (a seal in the US dub).
