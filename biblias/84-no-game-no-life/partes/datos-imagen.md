## Stephanie Dola · Appearance (texto de la wiki)
_Fuente: https://no-game-no-life.fandom.com/wiki/Stephanie_Dola#Appearance_

Stephanie is an Immanity girl with short red hair that is decorated with a large blue flower. She has blue eyes and a curvy figure with rather large breasts, and a dress by Sora that made Idols to be jealous. She wears a shoulder-less cardigan with a maid-like dress and a pair of leg warmers and boots. In Episode 1, she wore this elegant dress given to her by her grandfather. However, after losing it to Chlammy, she had to wear a towel before being dressed by Sora in a dress made from her bedsheet. Once the siblings beat Chlammy in a game for the crown of Immanity, Steph regained the dress and the dress became the main staple for her clothing thereafter. Her B-W-H sizes are 89, 58, 89.
