## Chrome · Appearance (texto de la wiki)
_Fuente: https://dr-stone.fandom.com/wiki/Chrome#Appearance_

Chrome is a young man of average height and build. He has round, brown eyes and matching spiky brown hair, and he keeps a rope tied around his head like a headband. He's most often seen with a cheerful expression. His usual outfit consists of a short-sleeved, dark blue shirt, matching pants, a rope belt, and two cross-body satchels that contain materials he's collected. When he was first introduced, Chrome donned a cape that matched the color of his outfit, hemmed with rope. Later in the series, his outfit it upgraded to less primitive satchels that have eyelets on the straps, and a shirt with the logo of the Kingdom of Science on the sleeves. His headband is also remade by Suika, and has more frayed edges.

Kohaku especially considers Suika adorable, and says so on several occasions. She has to wear a helmet made from a melon rind to help her see better because she has a disease called "blurry sickness", or in modern terms, near-sightedness. Senku created a pair of glass lens in her helmet to help her eyesight. She wears a dark-blue poncho that reaches to above her knees and wrists, and she has a large white rope attached to her neck like a necklace, which is common for her tribe, especially for girls.
