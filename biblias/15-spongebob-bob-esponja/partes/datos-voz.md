## Sheldon J. Plankton · Personality (texto de la wiki)
_Fuente: https://spongebob.fandom.com/wiki/Sheldon_J._Plankton#Personality_

Plankton is egotistical, aggressive, evil, and power-hungry. He is very jealous of Mr. Krabs and his success. His only goal throughout the series is stealing the Krabby Patty secret formula and running Krabs out of business, a desire that has led him to destroy the Krusty Krab on more than one occasion. He is manipulative, as he manipulates SpongeBob into becoming "assertive" in "Walking Small" to drive everyone in the beach away so he can build another Chum Bucket. He also manipulates SpongeBob in "F.U.N." by pretending to be his friend to get a Krabby Patty. He is also deceitful, as he is always trying to convince SpongeBob and the others that he can be trusted, only to use their goodwill to get closer to the formula. Either way, he is oftentimes too busy plotting revenge to interact with most of the characters. Being a villain might be lonely, but Plankton appears to like it that…
